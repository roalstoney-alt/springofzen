Parent FAQ

Is furniture included?

No. Current pages show digital concepts and illustrative experiences. They are not physical product listings.

What would I receive?

The exact images, guide, scope, timing, price and revision terms must be confirmed before any paid agreement. Do not infer a purchasable package from an illustration.

Can I build directly from the images?

No. They are not measured engineering plans. Use qualified local advice and manufacturer instructions.

Is AI used?

Some concept imagery is AI-assisted. It expresses design intent and requires review.

Do I need to send child information?

No. The adult inquiry form uses bounded room preferences and contact details. Do not submit child names, sensitive data or identifiable photographs.

Does an inquiry confirm an order?

No. A saved reference means the inquiry was stored, not that a service, payment or deadline has been agreed.

Where can I buy?

No live Etsy listing is confirmed on this site. Any future listing must be reviewed and published by the owner.
