Concept & Safety Limits

Room experiences and concept images illustrate design intent. Physical furniture, lighting, construction and installation are not included unless separately and explicitly agreed.

Concepts are not architectural, structural, electrical or certified child-safety engineering plans. Have appropriately qualified local professionals review measurements, products, stability, anchoring, ventilation, cords, lighting and applicable requirements.

Props, characters, light effects and staged room states do not demonstrate product availability, certification or a completed installation. Generated views can contain inconsistencies.

No medical, therapeutic, developmental, health or guaranteed sleep outcome is promised. Public imagery does not establish suitability for a particular age or child.
