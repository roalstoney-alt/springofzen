# Milo Plush SKU v0.1

Working ID: `SKU_OCEAN_001_MILO_PLUSH`  
Priority: P0  
Role: hero SKU, comfort object, physical-digital identity anchor, bedtime destination.

## Frozen prototype brief

- Soft, hug-friendly moon-blue whale silhouette consistent with digital Milo.
- Shared face language, eye placement, primary back marking, pale belly, and tail silhouette.
- No realistic anatomy, hard plastic parts, removable accessories, batteries, motors, camera, microphone, speaker, or Bluetooth.
- Stable resting orientation on a bed or bedside surface and easy visibility for room mapping.
- Prototype supplier brief must cover child-safe construction, washability, custom-character fidelity, repeatable dimensions, low-MOQ samples, and future packaging.

## Scene function

The plush is the destination of `MILO_RETURN_HOME_v0.1`. Digital Milo converges into its mapped position and disappears; the plush remains with one soft simulated glow cycle.

## Not yet frozen

Physical dimensions, materials, seam specification, compliance testing, care label, packaging, supplier, MOQ, and cost remain pending physical design and safety review. No inventory order is authorized.
