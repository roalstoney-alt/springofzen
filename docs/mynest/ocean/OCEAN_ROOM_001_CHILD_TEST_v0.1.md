# Ocean Room 001 Child Test v0.1

Status: protocol ready; no child result has been recorded.

## Participants

One or two children, ages 3–6, with a parent/caregiver present. Obtain appropriate pilot consent before structured data is sent. Publication consent remains separate and is not required.

## No-explanation protocol

Do not tell the child what to touch or what Milo will do. Start with the real room and observe whether the child independently:

- notices the low fish;
- touches or points near the floor/wall zone;
- notices digital Milo;
- activates the Shell Light or Reef Accent;
- recognizes the physical Milo Plush;
- watches Milo return into the plush;
- looks at, touches, or holds the plush afterward;
- requests a repeat or voluntarily returns.

## Identity-link observation

Do not ask “Are these the same Milo?” Record only observable signals: pointing between digital and physical Milo, using the same name for both, holding the plush after transfer, asking where Milo went, or waiting for Milo to return.

## Parent question after observation

Ask: “Would you want this room experience at home?” Then ask which part they would actually pay for: complete room project, Milo plus digital world, Shell Light, room kit, or none. Do not lead with price.

## Gate

Keep every child acceptance item at `NOT_TESTED` until a real physical-room session occurs. Browser interaction and adult review cannot produce a PASS for child discovery, identity link, return desire, or willingness to buy.
