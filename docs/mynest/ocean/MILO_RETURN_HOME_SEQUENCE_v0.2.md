# Milo Return Home Sequence v0.2

Purpose: end the experience by making digital Milo and the physical Milo Plush feel like one character.

## Timeline

1. Quiet (0–2.8 s): fish hide; water light and ambient sound reduce.
2. Turn (2.8–5.2 s): Milo notices the bed and turns toward the plush.
3. Travel (5.2–9.2 s): Milo follows `MILO_PATH_HOME`, crossing at normal scale before perspective slowly reduces it.
4. Transfer (9.2–12.4 s): local light gathers around the physical plush as the digital sprite softens and disappears into that exact anchor.
5. Sleep (12.4–18.2 s): two restrained breathing pulses occur; text appears only after the visual transfer: “Milo is sleeping.”

## Rules

- No early shrinking, teleport, full-room fade, or explanatory text during travel.
- Fish and interactive anchors become inactive during the sequence.
- The final frame is approximately 98% real room and 2% digital local light.
- Restart and Rest controls remain small and reversible.
- Reduced-motion mode preserves the same semantic phases with near-instant position changes and slow opacity transitions.

## Pass condition

A viewer must understand that Milo went into the plush and must notice the plush afterward without being told where Milo went. This requires a phone-video and no-explanation child test after physical calibration.
