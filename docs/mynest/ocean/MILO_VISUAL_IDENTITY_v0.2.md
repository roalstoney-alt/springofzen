# Milo Visual Identity v0.2

Milo is a young blue whale whose physical plush and digital resident share one unmistakable silhouette.

## Identity constants

- Slate-blue dorsal surface and warm ivory underside.
- Broad juvenile head, short rostrum, small dark eye, compact pectoral fins, and softly tapered flukes.
- Realistic skin texture with gentle indoor reflected light.
- Calm, curious posture; no fixed smile, brows, clothes, accessories, outline, or saturated cartoon color.
- No speech bubble, reward animation, or game-like callout.

## Living-room asset

`milo-living-cutout-v02.webp` is one clean transparent photographic resident asset. Idle, notice child, approach, play, return home, and sleep are expressed with restrained position, orientation, scale, light, and opacity changes so Milo's identity never shifts between generated poses.

Milo is composited with a weak floor contact light and soft shadow. Normal scene scale stays below 24% of desktop viewport width and 54% on portrait mobile. Entry is partially concealed near the shelf/bed region; Milo never arrives as a centered hero graphic.

## Behavior contract

The resident loop includes arrival, idle presence, voluntary departure, and return. A child can invite notice, approach, or a short play response, but cannot continuously steer Milo. Delayed and finite responses preserve agency.

At rest, the digital Milo travels to the real plush, transfers softly, and disappears. The physical plush is the only Milo that remains.
