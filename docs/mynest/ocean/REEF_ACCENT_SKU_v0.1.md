# Reef Accent SKU v0.1

Working ID: `SKU_OCEAN_003_REEF_ACCENT`  
Priority: P1  
Role: physical occlusion anchor and believable hiding place for small digital life.

## Frozen prototype brief

- Small abstract reef form suitable for a pale-oak bookshelf.
- Soft natural silhouette, rounded edges, quiet earth/ocean colour, stable base, and low visual clutter.
- No large fake coral, bright plastic toy treatment, electronics, or continuous animation.
- Prototype material must be non-toxic, stable on the shelf, and suitable for repeat manufacture.

## Scene function

Fish approach the lower shelf, disappear behind the mapped reef boundary, and one small crab-like creature briefly peeks out. The event lasts about five seconds and then returns to idle.

## Not yet frozen

Dimensions, weight, final material, tooling, attachment method, compliance testing, supplier, MOQ, and cost remain pending physical prototype review. No inventory order is authorized.
