## Primary framing

**Own-room night setup**

Start with what the child needs tonight: one low light, one sound choice, and one familiar daytime object or room friend. The room stays ordinary; the digital world quietly brings familiar things to life.

## Product principle

You do not need an ocean-themed bedroom. You need a room, a light, a familiar object, and a room friend. Furniture remains neutral scene architecture and is not offered as product.
