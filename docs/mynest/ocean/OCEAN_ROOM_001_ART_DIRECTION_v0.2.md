# Ocean Room 001 — Art Direction v0.2

Status: implemented web-scene direction; physical-room calibration remains pending.

## Visual thesis

Ocean Room 001 is a believable, warm child's bedroom first and a digital habitat second. The room remains approximately 85% physical and 15% digital while active, then returns to approximately 98% physical in the final sleep state. Empty floor and wall space are intentional.

## Camera and composition

- Camera height: approximately 100 cm, within the required 90–110 cm child-eye band.
- Desktop master: 16:9, 1672 × 941 source; window and linen curtain left, low shelf center-left, small bedside surface and shell light, low bed right.
- Mobile master: dedicated 9:16, 941 × 1672 source; not a desktop crop. Clear floor remains the largest interactive region.
- Palette: warm cream, pale oak, quiet slate blue, weak sea-glass accents.
- Natural window light is dominant. Digital light must never recolor the whole room blue.

## Frozen physical inventory

Only these MyNest products appear: Milo Plush, Shell Light, and Reef Accent. The low bed, shelf, bedside surface, window, curtain, a few books, and one storage box are ordinary room furniture.

Prohibited decor includes plants, generic lamps, bears, toy houses, boats, whale wall art, baskets, large book collections, and any additional ocean merchandise.

## Digital occupancy

- Default fish: 3; hard maximum: 5.
- Milo: one juvenile whale resident.
- Digital life stays below the room's midline except for brief, purposeful Milo transit.
- Water light is a weak floor/lower-wall reflection, never a ceiling treatment or full-room transformation.
- One authored interaction event may run at a time.

## Acceptance reference

With all overlays disabled, the image must look like a tasteful room a parent could build. With overlays enabled, a viewer should say that fish are in the room—not that the room has ocean wallpaper or is playing a projector video.
