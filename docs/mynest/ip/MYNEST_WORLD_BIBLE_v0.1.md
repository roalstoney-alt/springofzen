# MyNest World Bible v0.1

Status: frozen for `MYNEST_MAGIC_ROOM_COHORT_001`
Working world and character names remain subject to trademark and IP review.

## Premise

MyNest is a child-owned responsive room. It is not a projector toy, medical
device, sleep therapy, or decoration package. The room should communicate:
"my world lives here."

The product primitive is:

```text
SPACE + LIGHT + IMAGE + SOUND + CHARACTER + STORY + INTERACTION + CHOICE
= CHILD-OWNED WORLD
```

## Shared engine

Every world uses the same eight states:

```text
ENTRY → AWAKEN → DISCOVER → INTERACT → STORY → WIND_DOWN → SLEEP → RETURN
```

- Entry waits without demanding attention.
- Awaken establishes world identity and the lead character.
- Discover gives one observable reason to explore.
- Interact gives bounded agency without frantic stimulation.
- Story uses stable geography and character behavior.
- Wind Down reduces motion, luminance, and audio together.
- Sleep is near-static and contains no play prompt.
- Return leaves one recognizable reason to come back tomorrow.

## 80/20 IP rule

Fixed IP (80%): characters, personalities, relationships, visual grammar,
landmarks, world rules, story canon, core sound language, and bedtime logic.

Bounded personalization (20%): optional child nickname, favorite color, chosen
companion, daily sequence, birthday event, and selected bedtime world. Core
world identity must never be replaced by unrestricted generation.

## Visual and interaction grammar

- Movement is slow, legible, and spatially connected to the room.
- A lead character must remain recognizable across state changes.
- One interaction produces one clear response.
- The room never uses rapid flashing, strobing, startling cuts, or high-intensity
  light directed near a child's eyes.
- Interaction may invite; it must not command, punish, shame, or keep a child
  awake.
- Sleep state removes rewards, tasks, countdowns, and return prompts.

## Character contract

Every character file defines:

```yaml
character_id:

species_or_type:
role:
personality:
visual_signature:
movement_style:
sound_signature:
relationship_to_child:
relationship_to_other_characters:
play_behavior:
bedtime_behavior:
repeat_phrase:
```

Characters behave consistently across stories. At least one character per
world may later bridge digital and physical form, but physical manufacturing is
outside v0.1.

## Daily event contract

One event should be understandable in under one minute, create one child-owned
action, resolve without purchase, and leave one tomorrow cue. Events must not
turn sleep state back into play state.

## MyNest Moment contract

Each world owns one 8–12 second transformation that is recognizable in an
unedited phone video. Recording is optional. Pilot rewards and access never
depend on filming, posting, reviewing, or publishing.

## Audio language

Audio remains low, nonverbal by default, and state-linked. Play may contain a
simple motif; Wind Down removes high-frequency detail; Sleep keeps only a
steady, low-level ambience or silence. The parent always controls sound.

## Forbidden design patterns

- generic interchangeable skins;
- unbounded AI character or story replacement;
- surveillance or recording by default;
- engagement loops during sleep;
- escalating brightness, speed, or volume to compensate for weak attachment;
- medical or independent-sleep claims;
- prompts that make a child responsible for pilot success;
- UGC consent bundled with pilot consent.
