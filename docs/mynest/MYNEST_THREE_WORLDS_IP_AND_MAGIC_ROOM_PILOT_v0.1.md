# 1. Core Strategic Decision

MyNest is no longer defined primarily as:

```text
Own-Room Transition Solution
```

It is upgraded to:

> **A child-owned responsive room that can become a world of its own.**

Own-room transition remains the first high-value use case, but the product itself must generate attraction independently of parental persuasion.

The core hypothesis is:

> If a child's room becomes a responsive, persistent, character-driven world that the child feels belongs to them, the room itself may create enough attraction to increase voluntary entry, voluntary return, and eventually willingness to sleep there.

This is the central MyNest product hypothesis.
