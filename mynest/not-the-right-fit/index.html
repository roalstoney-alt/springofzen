SAFETY AND SCOPE

When MyNest is not the right fit

Pause the room experiment when the concern may need more than an environmental or bedtime-routine change. MyNest does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for medical, developmental, or mental-health care.

Pause and seek appropriate qualified guidance when there are

  • breathing concerns;
  • significant or persistent pain;
  • severe distress;
  • persistent disruptive nightmares or night terrors;
  • strong daytime fear connected to sleep or the room;
  • major developmental or medical concerns; or
  • unsafe room conditions.

What pausing means

Do not force entry, lock a door, use punishment, or continue an experiment that is making distress worse. Keep the child safe and contact an appropriate pediatric, medical, child-development, or mental-health professional for guidance relevant to the concern.

If none of these boundaries applies but the 14-day observation shows no clear change, review the pattern without treating the child or parent as having failed. You can stop, return to the familiar arrangement, or discuss the pattern with an appropriate professional.
