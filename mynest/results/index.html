Outcome definitions

A full own-room night is a full night slept in the child's own room. A partial own-room night is part, but not all, of a night. An own-room attempt records full-night, partial-night, or entered-only attempts; a partial night is never counted as a full night. Voluntary room entry records whether entry was easy, supported, or did not occur. The complete frozen definitions are on the Method page.

Publication gate

A future public case may contain only an anonymous case ID, age band, baseline, structured barrier and child choice, Day 1/3/7/14 outcomes, and limitations. It must represent a completed real family and pass completion, consent, privacy, and evidence checks. Test or synthetic records are excluded.

What is never public

Child names, parent names, email addresses, phone numbers, exact birth dates, photos, addresses, private account IDs, and free-text notes.

Evidence limits

  • Observations are caregiver reported and household specific.
  • The current protocol is an early pilot without a validated clinical outcome measure.
  • No causal, therapeutic, predictive, or population-level conclusion can be drawn.
  • Product price, availability, shipping, safety certification, and commercial readiness are NOT_ESTABLISHED.
